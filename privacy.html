Privacy Policy

Ed HQ · Last updated October 3, 2026

What Ed HQ is

Ed HQ is a personal assistant application built and used only by Edward Moradian. It is not offered to the public, and no other users can sign in to it.

Data it accesses

With Edward's permission through Google sign-in, Ed HQ may read and organize his own Gmail messages (for example, searching, labeling, and archiving) and read or manage his own Google Calendar events. It accesses only the account that granted permission.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to carry out Edward's own requests. It is processed on his personal computer and is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Google user data is handled in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/connections. Revoking access stops Ed HQ from reading any further data.
